Privacy Policy

Game: Blocks (Android)  ·  Developer: RAGS Studio  ·  Effective: 22 October 2026

This policy explains what information Blocks collects, why, and the choices you have. We have tried to keep it short and plain.

1. Who we are

Blocks is made by RAGS Studio, an independent game developer based in Queensland, Australia. You can contact us at rags.studio.26@gmail.com.

2. Information that stays on your device

The game saves your progress on your phone: your best score, your coins, the themes you own and have equipped, your settings (sound, music, vibration), and any run in progress so you can resume it. We do not create an account for you, and we do not ask for your name, email address or any other personal details. This saved data is not sent to us. It is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data in Android settings.

3. Information collected by services we use

Blocks uses third-party services. They may collect information from your device while you play, as described below.

  • Advertising (Google AdMob). The game can show ads, including optional ads you choose to watch in return for a reward. Google's advertising SDK may collect and use your device's advertising ID, your IP address (which can indicate a rough location), how you interact with the app (such as launches, taps and ad views), and diagnostic information (such as app launch time and crashes). This is used to show and measure ads, and for fraud prevention. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase). We use Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to understand how the game is used and to find and fix crashes. They may collect app instance identifiers, device and app information (such as device model, Android version and app version), anonymous usage events (such as when a run starts or ends) and crash reports. See Firebase privacy information.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Consent and your choices

  • If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the game asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, using Google's consent message. You can change your choice at any time from the settings screen, where available.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, and opt out of ad personalisation, in your phone's Settings under Google → Ads.
  • You can play without watching any ads. Ads are used only for the optional reward feature.

5. Children

Blocks is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the game, contact us at the address above and we will help remove it.

6. Keeping and deleting data

Data on your device is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its storage. Analytics and crash data held by Google services is kept and deleted according to their policies linked above. To ask us about data connected to you, email us and we will respond as soon as we reasonably can.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when we add a new feature or service. The effective date at the top shows when it was last changed.
